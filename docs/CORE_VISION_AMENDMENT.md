# DOME: OZARK EXTINCTION
## Core Vision Amendment — World Reclamation System

**Status:** Binding amendment to the North Star  
**Date recorded:** 2026-07-30

All future development decisions must be evaluated against these principles.

---

## PURPOSE

Defines deeper narrative, technical priorities, visual standards, and long-term franchise architecture.

The North Star remains the constitution.  
This amendment adds **non-negotiable** commercial and franchise requirements that must not be lost when work is broken into tasks.

---

## 1. VISUAL QUALITY STANDARD

### Creature Quality Is A Primary Selling Point

Dinosaurs and prehistoric creatures are the face of the game.

They **must not** feel like:

- low-poly placeholders (in final builds)
- arcade enemies
- generic AI models
- outdated animations

**Target:** A creature experience dramatically beyond the original Carnivores: Ice Age inspiration.

The player should feel **fear, scale, realism, and wonder**.  
The first time a player sees a dinosaur should feel like seeing a living animal — not a game asset.

### Creature Requirements (Major Species)

**Visual systems**

- realistic anatomy
- detailed skin
- muscle movement
- breathing, blinking, facial animation
- natural movement
- environmental interaction

**Animation quality**

- walking, running, feeding, drinking, sleeping
- attacking, fleeing, investigating, territorial behavior
- no robotic movement

**Behavior over graphics alone**

Realism comes from behavior. A T-Rex should not simply attack. It should:

- patrol territory
- investigate sounds
- follow prey
- become frustrated
- retreat
- protect territory

### Prototype vs Final

Temporary/procedural assets are allowed during vertical-slice development.  
**Do not design final systems around low-quality placeholders.**  
Architecture and pipelines must support high-fidelity creatures without a rewrite.

---

## 2. ZERO-COST DEVELOPMENT REQUIREMENT

### Philosophy

Build with a **"no money until necessary"** approach.

Reach the highest possible playable quality before spending money.

### Prefer

- free engines and tools
- open-source software
- free / AI-assisted asset creation
- procedural generation

### Spending Approval Rule

Before any expense, ask:

1. Does this directly improve the game?
2. Is there a free alternative?
3. Is this required for launch?
4. Does this increase sales potential?

### Acceptable future costs (when required)

- Steam publishing fee
- domain
- required legal services
- paid assets that significantly improve quality
- server costs only if multiplayer requires them

Avoid unnecessary spending before validation.

## 3. THE DOME CLEARING STORY ARC

### Core Narrative

Humanity is **not** trying to survive forever inside the domes.  
Humanity is trying to **reclaim Earth**.

Domes are containment zones created after prehistoric life overwhelmed the planet.

The governing organization cannot simply bomb, poison, or bulldoze ecosystems — Earth must remain habitable.

**Solution:** specialized hunters.

### The Hunter Role

The player is **not a soldier**.  
The player is a specialist selected because they:

- understand wildlife
- can move silently
- can survive alone
- can remove apex threats without destroying ecosystems

### Dome Objective

Each dome must be:

1. Entered
2. Studied
3. Cleared
4. Stabilized
5. Returned to humanity

### Dome Completion Event

When the player clears a dome:

> **Player radio:** "Dome ecosystem stabilized. Apex threats removed. Zone ready for reclamation."  
> **Command:** "Confirmed hunter. Extraction authorized."

### Extraction Cinematic (Signature Franchise Moment)

1. Helicopter arrives  
2. Player attaches final trophy  
3. Helicopter lifts; player hangs from the side  
4. Dome wall opens  
5. Helicopter rises above the landscape  
6. **The Reveal:** thousands of domes across continents — different colors, shapes, sizes, environments  
7. Realization: **The Ozark Dome was only the beginning.**

**This moment must be preserved.** Do not cut it for scope convenience.

### Global Dome Map (Post-Ozark)

- Cleared Domes — safe zones
- Active Domes — current missions
- Unknown Domes — unexplored
- Extreme Domes — high difficulty

---

## 4. DOME EVOLUTION SYSTEM

A cleared dome changes permanently.

| Before | After |
|--------|--------|
| Wild containment zone | Human reclamation zone |

Cleared domes become: landing zones, military bases, research stations, survivor settlements, supply hubs.

**Player impact:** early game is empty wilderness; later the world shows lights, structures, vehicles, towers — humanity returning.

---

## 5. FUTURE MULTIPLAYER ARCHITECTURE

Do **not** implement multiplayer initially.  
Architecture **must not prevent** it.

Future concept — **Hunter Network**:

- clear domes, claim territories, build command centers
- contribute to global reclamation
- player-owned domes → HQ, storage, research, hubs

Progression fantasy: Individual hunter → Dome commander → Regional commander → Planetary reclamation force.

---

## 6. FUTURE TRANSPORT & BETWEEN-DOMES

Later: travel between domes (helicopters, aircraft, ships).

Space between domes = true wild Earth (not safe):

- sky threats (e.g. Quetzalcoatlus, giant predatory birds)
- ground: uncontrolled ecosystems, invasive plants, hostile wildlife
- extreme survival zones: poisonous/carnivorous plants, storms, migrating predators

---

## 7. LONG-TERM CONTENT MODEL

Endless expansion via new domes, e.g.:

Desert · Jungle · Swamp · Ice Age · Ocean · Volcanic · Alien Ecosystem

Each adds creatures, environments, mechanics, trophies, challenges.

---

## 8. NON-NEGOTIABLE CHECKLIST (North Star Requirements)

Mark these as **non-negotiable** in production tools:

1. Creature visual quality is a primary selling point
2. Avoid low-quality placeholder creatures in final builds
3. Zero-cost development methods until spending is absolutely required
4. Ozark Dome is only the first chapter of global reclamation
5. Dome clearing is the core progression system
6. Helicopter extraction reveal is a signature story moment — preserve it
7. Architecture must support additional domes, multiplayer reclamation, player-owned bases, global progression
8. Systems must be modular so content can be added without rebuilding the game
