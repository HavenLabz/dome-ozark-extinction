Field Report

The ecosystem does not wait for you.

DOME isn't a shooting gallery. It's a living Ozark wilderness — real oak-hickory forest, limestone bluffs, spring-fed rivers and karst caves — rebuilt faithfully, then seeded with what should have stayed extinct. Deer and black bear share the valley with a Triceratops herd and a raptor pack that hunts as one. You are not the apex predator here. You're an intruder with a rifle and a radio.

Do not break containment
Mission Directive

Enter the sealed dome as the last expedition cleared for entry.

Document the species — real modern fauna and resurrected megafauna alike.

Hunt what you can handle; the T‑Rex and Spinosaurus can handle you.

Recover a trophy, pop the flare, and survive until extraction touches down.

Reclaim the dome — the first of many. Earth is waiting.

Core Systems

Everything a hunt needs, nothing that breaks the spell.

Wildlife-first AI

Animals, not enemies

Creatures see, hear, roam their territory, flee, investigate, and pack-hunt. Sprint and they hear you from twice as far.

Tracking

Read the wilderness

Fresh footprints fade over time. Glass a ridge through binoculars to read a species, its threat, and its trophy value before you commit.

Ballistics

Real weapons

AR‑15 and M1911 with ammo, reload, recoil and true down-the-sights aim. Creatures aren't bullet sponges — placement matters.

Survival

The dome fights back

Hunger, hydration and stamina drain. Drink from the river, harvest meat, and hold a campfire haven through the night.

Day / Night

Nightfall changes everything

A full day-night cycle rolls over the valley. After dark it's colder, foggier, and far more dangerous.

Extraction

The signature moment

Down a trophy, deploy your flare, and survive the wait — then the helicopter drops in and lifts you out. That's the run.
